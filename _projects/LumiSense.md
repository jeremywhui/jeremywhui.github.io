A common issue many individuals face is waking up feeling tired, even after getting the recommended 7 - 9 hours of sleep. Scientific research suggests that this often results from waking during the deeper stages of sleep, rather than during the lighter stages. Existing solutions attempt to address this issue using mobile applications or through smart wearable devices. These tools typically track motion or sound to estimate sleep stages, but come with drawbacks of being expensive, requiring direct physical contact with the user, or necessitate the placement of a sensor on the sleeping surface.

To address these limitations, we propose LumiSense: a low-cost, non-intrusive device that estimates sleep stage progression using motion signals. LumiSense consists of an attachable nano-computer: the Adafruit Circuit Playground Bluefruit, which includes built-in motion and sound sensors. The app will log and display sleep metrics such as total sleep time and estimated time spent in each sleep stage. Users can also specify an earliest and latest acceptable wake-up time within a 30 minute period, and the app will trigger the alarm at the most optimal moment within that window—when the user is in the lightest sleep stage, as determined by signal activity and machine learning predictions. LumiSense offers a user-friendly and affordable alternative to current sleep-tracking technologies. By integrating motion data with a lightweight machine learning model, LumiSense aims to enhance the accuracy of sleep stage predictions and improve morning wakefulness.
